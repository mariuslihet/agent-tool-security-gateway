What happens during a pause?

Execution guardrails stay in force

  • Agents receive a structured, retryable TENANT_PAUSED response.
  • Paused attempts do not consume execution budgets or approval tokens.
  • Existing cached outcomes remain available.
  • On expiry or resume, every normal authorization check still runs.
  • Revocations, data labels, uncertain outcomes and policy denials are retained.

Operator access is required to change containment.
